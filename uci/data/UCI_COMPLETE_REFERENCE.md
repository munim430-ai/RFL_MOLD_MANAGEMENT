### Korean Language Requirement (TOPIK — Test of Proficiency in Korean)

| Level | 한글 | Who Needs It | Notes |
|-------|------|-------------|-------|
| TOPIK I — Level 1 | 초급 | Language school / D-4 visa applicants | Basic survival Korean |
| TOPIK I — Level 2 | 초급 | Language school completion | Basic conversation |
| TOPIK II — Level 3 | 중급 | **Minimum for Korean-taught university programs (D-2 visa)** | Most common requirement |
| TOPIK II — Level 4 | 중급 | Preferred for competitive programs | Business Korean |
| TOPIK II — Level 5 | 고급 | Graduate programs, research | Near-fluent |
| TOPIK II — Level 6 | 고급 | Top universities / scholarships | Fluent |

> **Note for Bangladeshi students:** TOPIK exam is held in Dhaka (Korean Embassy / designated centres).
> English-taught programs at Korean universities do **not** require TOPIK — IELTS 5.5–6.5 is accepted instead.

### Korea D-2 / D-4 Visa — Quick Reference

| Item | Requirement |
|------|------------|
| **Visa Type** | D-2 (University Study) or D-4 (Language Training) |
| **Admission Letter** | Required from Korean university / language institute |
| **Bank Balance** | USD $20,000 equivalent (~KRW 27,000,000) — 30-day certificate |
| **TOPIK** | Level 3+ for Korean-taught programs; not required for English-taught |
| **Academic Docs** | SSC + HSC transcripts, notarized and apostilled |
| **Health Certificate** | Required |
| **Processing Time** | 4–8 weeks (Korean Embassy Dhaka) |
| **Part-time Work** | 20 hrs/week during semester; unlimited during vacation |
| **GKS Scholarship** | Government scholarship deadline: Feb–March annually |
| **Visa Fee** | ~USD $60 |

### South Korea — D-2 / D-4 Visa ★

**Governing Law:** Immigration Act of Korea; Enforcement Decree of the Immigration Act

#### Visa Types
| Type | Purpose | Duration |
|------|---------|----------|
| **D-4** | Korean language training at language institute | Up to 1 year (renewable) |
| **D-2-1** | Associate degree program | Duration of program |
| **D-2-2** | Bachelor's degree | Duration of program |
| **D-2-3** | Master's degree | Duration of program |
| **D-2-4** | Doctoral degree | Duration of program |
| **D-2-6** | Exchange student | Duration of exchange |
| **D-2-7** | Specific research/training | Duration |

#### Requirements — Bangladeshi Applicants
| Document | Specification |
|----------|--------------|
| Admission letter | From NIIED-recognized or MOE-approved university |
| Financial proof | USD $20,000 (~KRW 27,000,000) — bank statement (30 days minimum) |
| Academic records | SSC + HSC mark sheets; degree certificates if graduate |
| Notarization | All academic docs must be notarized + apostilled |
| Korean proficiency | TOPIK Level 3+ for Korean-taught; IELTS 5.5+ for English-taught |
| Health certificate | Medical exam from designated clinic |
| Passport validity | Minimum 6 months beyond intended stay |
| Application form | Form 34 (Unified visa application) |

#### Financial Requirements Detail
| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Proof of funds (living) | USD $20,000 (KRW ~27,000,000) |
| Tuition (public university) | KRW 3,000,000–5,000,000/semester (~USD 2,200–3,700) |
| Tuition (private university) | KRW 4,000,000–7,000,000/semester (~USD 3,000–5,200) |
| Dormitory | KRW 300,000–600,000/month (~USD 220–450) |
| Living expenses | KRW 600,000–1,000,000/month (~USD 450–750) |

#### Key Regulations
- Part-time work allowed: **20 hours/week** during semester; **unlimited** during vacation (D-2 visa holders)
- D-4 holders: **25 hours/week** after 6 months of attendance with >80% attendance record
- **GKS (Global Korea Scholarship):** Full funding (tuition + living + airfare); apply Feb–March
- **NIIED Exchange Scholarship:** For exchange students at partner universities
- Change of status (D-4 → D-2) allowed without leaving Korea if admitted to degree program
- Registration: Must register at local immigration office within **90 days** of entry

### United Kingdom — Student Route Visa

**Governing Law:** Immigration Rules (Appendix Student); Immigration Act 1971; UK Borders Act 2007

#### Requirements
| Document | Specification |
